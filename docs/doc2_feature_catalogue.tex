\section{MICRO FEATURES — Always Free}
\textit{Core features accessible to every farmer, zero cost, zero app required}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{featurebox}{primaryGreen}{F-01 \quad Hyperlocal 1-km Temperature Forecast \quad \freebadge}
Real-time downscaled temperature for the farmer's exact 1-km grid cell — not a district average. Powered by Model 1 (Random Forest + AWS calibration).

\textbf{Impact:} Farmers know if their field will be 2°C cooler than the town forecast — critical for frost-sensitive crops like tomato and mango.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{primaryGreen}{F-02 \quad Daily Rain Yes/No Alert \quad \freebadge}
Simple go/no-go rainfall prediction for the next 24 hours delivered via SMS or WhatsApp. Model 3 hurdle classifier outputs rain probability; threshold-based binary alert generated automatically.

\textbf{Impact:} Prevents unnecessary irrigation on a day when rain is coming — saves water, electricity, and diesel pump costs.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{primaryGreen}{F-03 \quad 3-Day Basic Weather Forecast \quad \freebadge}
Temperature high/low, humidity, and wind speed for 3 days at 1-km resolution, displayed in Hindi on a simple card view. No internet required beyond initial SMS.

\textbf{Impact:} Helps farmers plan field operations (spraying, harvesting, sowing) 3 days in advance.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{primaryGreen}{F-04 \quad WhatsApp \& SMS Advisory Delivery \quad \freebadge}
Zero-app-install distribution. All advisories are delivered as plain Hindi text via WhatsApp Business API or SMS gateway — works on any ₹500 feature phone.

\textbf{Impact:} Eliminates smartphone adoption as a barrier. Reaches the bottom 60\% of farmers who do not own smartphones.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{primaryGreen}{F-05 \quad Panchayat-Level Location Tagging \quad \freebadge}
All forecasts are tagged to the farmer's specific panchayat (one of 5 pilot panchayats in Lucknow → 6,000+ across UP). No GPS required — farmer registers their village name once.

\textbf{Impact:} Replaces generic district forecasts with village-level accuracy.
\end{featurebox}

\section{SMALL-MEDIUM FEATURES — Kisan Saathi (₹49/month)}
\textit{Operational intelligence for input optimisation and daily farm management}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{featurebox}{blue}{F-06 \quad Soil Moisture Status \quad \saathibadge}
Surface and root-zone soil moisture estimate for the farmer's 1-km cell, fusing Sentinel-1 SAR backscatter with ISRIC SoilGrids soil texture data (Model 4).

\textbf{Impact:} Tells farmer directly: ``Your field's root zone has 24\% volumetric moisture — this is above the irrigation threshold for paddy. Skip irrigation today.'' Saves 2--4 irrigation events per crop cycle.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{blue}{F-07 \quad Irrigation Scheduling Engine \quad \saathibadge}
Daily irrigation requirement in mm computed using FAO-56 Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration, calibrated with 1-km temperature, humidity, and wind (from M1/M2) and soil moisture deficit (M4). Output: ``Irrigate 35 mm tomorrow morning. Next required: Oct 7.''

\textbf{Impact:} 15--25\% water saving per season. In UP where groundwater depletion is critical, this is both economic and environmental.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{blue}{F-08 \quad 7-Day Extended Forecast \quad \saathibadge}
Full 7-day hyperlocal forecast at 1-km resolution — temperature, humidity, rainfall probability, wind, and solar radiation. Enables weekly farm planning.

\textbf{Impact:} Farmers can plan labour booking, equipment hire, and market trips a week in advance.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{rose}{F-09 \quad \textbf{Weather-Triggered Fertiliser Alert} \quad \saathibadge\quad\newbadge}
The platform monitors the 7-day precipitation forecast for each panchayat. When an optimal fertiliser application window is detected — soil is moist enough for uptake but rain is 4--7 days away (preventing runoff) — an automatic SMS/push alert is sent.

\textbf{Example Message (Hindi):}
\begin{mdframed}[backgroundcolor=cardBg,linecolor=primaryGreen,linewidth=1pt,roundcorner=4pt]
\textit{``मालीहाबाद: आपके क्षेत्र में 6 दिन बाद वर्षा की संभावना है। DAP/यूरिया डालने का यह उत्तम समय है। 3 अक्टूबर से पहले डालें। भारी बारिश से 48 घंटे पहले खाद डालने से बचें।''}\\[4pt]
\textit{(Translation: Malihabad: Rain expected in 6 days in your region. This is the optimal time to apply DAP/Urea. Apply before Oct 3. Avoid application within 48 hours of heavy rainfall.)}
\end{mdframed}

\textbf{Impact:} Prevents ₹2,000--8,000/acre loss per season from nutrient runoff caused by fertiliser application immediately before heavy rain. Also prevents the opposite — applying in too-dry soil where uptake is poor.\\
\textbf{Monetisation:} Agri-input companies (IFFCO, Coromandel) can sponsor targeted fertiliser alerts as precision advertising — revenue neutral for the farmer.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{blue}{F-10 \quad Pesticide/Spray Timing Advisor \quad \saathibadge}
Checks rainfall forecast before recommending pesticide or fungicide spray. Spraying within 4 hours of rain = 100\% chemical washoff = wasted input. System sends alert only when a dry window of 6+ hours is confirmed.

\textbf{Impact:} Saves ₹500--1,500/acre per spray event. Reduces chemical load on soil and water bodies.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{blue}{F-11 \quad Crop Stage Tracking (Growth Calendar) \quad \saathibadge}
NDVI/EVI time series from Sentinel-2 tracks crop growth stage (emergence, tillering, booting, heading, maturity) and maps to Growing Degree Day (GDD) accumulation. Farmer receives: ``Your wheat is at tillering stage. 47 GDD accumulated. Top-dress nitrogen now.''

\textbf{Impact:} Prevents split-dose misapplication — one of the top causes of poor yield in wheat and paddy.
\end{featurebox}

\section{LARGE FEATURES — Kisan Pro (₹89/month)}
\textit{Strategic intelligence — protect the entire season, not just tomorrow}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{featurebox}{purple}{F-12 \quad Extreme Weather Hazard Alerts (48--72h Advance) \quad \probadge}
Model 9 detects anomalous atmospheric events against 30-year ERA5 climatology baselines:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Heatwave:} Temperature $>$ 95th percentile for 3+ consecutive days
  \item \textbf{Cold Wave / Frost Risk:} T $<$ 4°C during Rabi season (wheat, mustard, potato)
  \item \textbf{Prolonged Dry Spell:} Zero rainfall $>$ 7 days during Kharif
  \item \textbf{Waterlogging Risk:} Cumulative rainfall $>$ field capacity + impermeable layer detected
  \item \textbf{Loo (hot dry wind):} Wind speed + temperature + humidity index during May--June
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Impact:} A 48-hour advance frost alert allows a mango farmer in Malihabad to apply protective smoke, cover young shoots, or arrange supplemental heating — preventing 60--100\% crop loss that would otherwise occur overnight.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{purple}{F-13 \quad Crop Yield Forecast \quad \probadge}
End-of-season yield estimate (quintals/hectare) computed using cumulative weather anomalies, vegetation stress index, and irrigation history — calibrated against district-level CCE (Crop Cutting Experiment) data (Model 7).

\textbf{Impact:} Farmers can forward-sell at better prices if yield looks good. FPOs can negotiate bulk procurement contracts. Insurance companies get objective pre-harvest yield estimates.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{purple}{F-14 \quad Flood \& Waterlogging Risk Map \quad \probadge}
TWI (Topographic Wetness Index) + DEM depression analysis identifies 1-km cells at risk of waterlogging when M3 precipitation forecast crosses field-capacity thresholds. Generates 24--72 hour inundation risk maps per panchayat (Model 8).

\textbf{Impact:} Paddy farmers in low-lying fields can pre-open drainage channels and prevent root asphyxiation. Saves 15--40\% yield in high-risk years.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{rose}{F-15 \quad Hindi Voice Advisory (IVR Phone Call) \quad \probadge\quad\newbadge}
For farmers who cannot read SMS, an automated IVR (Interactive Voice Response) outbound call delivers the same advisory in spoken Hindi. Delivery triggered automatically when a critical alert is issued.

\textbf{Example call script:} \textit{``Namaste! Yeh Kisaan Ki Yash ki taraf se ek zaroori sandesh hai. Kal aapke kshetra mein tez barish hone ki sambhavana hai. Kripya aaj hi apni fasal ki kutti kaaten. Dhanyavaad.''}

\textbf{Impact:} Extends reach to illiterate and elderly farmers — the demographic most financially vulnerable to crop loss.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{purple}{F-16 \quad PMFBY Insurance Report Auto-Generation \quad \probadge}
Verified M1/M9 weather event records (hailstorm, flood, drought) are formatted into structured crop damage evidence reports compatible with PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) claim submission requirements.

\textbf{Impact:} Eliminates the need for contested field inspections. Objective, timestamped, satellite-verified evidence accelerates insurance claim settlement from months to days.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{purple}{F-17 \quad Conformal Prediction Intervals \quad \probadge}
Every temperature and rainfall forecast comes with scientifically computed uncertainty bounds (90\% conformal prediction intervals): ``Temperature: 28.4°C [27.5°C — 29.3°C]''. Bounds are honestly withheld when calibration data is insufficient — system abstains rather than fabricates confidence.

\textbf{Impact:} Farmers and agronomists can see how confident the system is. High uncertainty = decision deferred. Low uncertainty = act now.
\end{featurebox}

\section{ENTERPRISE FEATURES — FPO / Business (₹499/month)}
\textit{Portfolio-level intelligence for Farmer Producer Organisations, cooperatives, and agri-businesses}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{featurebox}{amber}{F-18 \quad Multi-Farm Dashboard \quad \bizzbadge}
Aggregate view of all enrolled farms in the FPO — panchayat-level heatmaps of soil moisture, yield forecast, and risk alerts. Export to Excel or PDF for management meetings.

\textbf{Impact:} An FPO managing 500 farmer members can see at a glance which blocks need emergency intervention vs.\ which are performing well.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{amber}{F-19 \quad REST API Access \quad \bizzbadge}
Full programmatic access to all 10 model outputs via documented REST API endpoints. Enables integration with ERP systems, procurement platforms, and custom dashboards.

\textbf{Impact:} Agri-input companies, seed companies, and rural banks can embed live farm intelligence into their own workflows.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{amber}{F-20 \quad e-NAM Price Signal Integration \quad \bizzbadge\quad\newbadge}
Yield forecast (M7) is cross-referenced with real-time e-NAM (National Agricultural Market) commodity prices to generate optimal harvest-timing recommendations: ``Harvest within next 10 days to capture current high-price window before new-season arrivals depress market.''

\textbf{Impact:} Farmers sell at peak prices instead of distress-selling during harvest glut. 10--25\% income uplift on market-connected farms.
\end{featurebox}

\begin{featurebox}{amber}{F-21 \quad Carbon Credit Verification Reports \quad \bizzbadge\quad\newbadge}
Precision irrigation scheduling (M6) + verified soil moisture data (M4) enable measurement of water savings and reduced groundwater extraction. Formatted as Verra/Gold Standard carbon credit methodology-compatible MRV reports.

\textbf{Impact:} An FPO managing 1,000 acres can potentially earn ₹5--15 lakh/year in voluntary carbon market credits — additional income stream on top of crop revenue.
\end{featurebox}
